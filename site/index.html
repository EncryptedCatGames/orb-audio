Honest limits: ORB synthesizes, it does not record, so a real door slam still wins. 47 roles and 13 beds, no music, no voices or animal calls yet. The planner reads its caption grammar exactly and plain words on a best-effort basis (about 60–70% of test prompts planned fully right; for a sound it has no role for, it may invent the nearest one); the second-opinion helper is plain keyword rules. The detail model is subtle: it passes the no-noise gate but misses its 10% restoration target (+0.84%), and takes tens of seconds per cue on a CPU.
